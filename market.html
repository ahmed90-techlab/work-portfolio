A low-cost, high-impact approach to entering the African market

建立非洲直接市场联系
低成本、高回报的非洲市场切入方案

The Problem
  • We cannot physically visit Africa to meet potential buyers.
  • Cold emails and remote research are not enough to build trust.
  • We need real feedback from the African market before investing in a full sales push.
The Solution / Core Idea
  • Use trusted local friends/contacts in Africa to visit target companies on our behalf.
  • We collect senior-level contacts, arrange product samples, and set up virtual meetings.
My Experience at Broadlink

At my previous company (Smart Home Industry), we faced some challenges:

  • Our smart products were sold in North America / Europe / India.
  • We needed testing feedback (bugs, connection failures, etc.) from different locations.
  • I asked my Chinese/foreign friends studying/living in those markets.
  • I offered them a part-time testing role.
  • They tested our products & reported issues directly to us.
How It Works
Action Outcome
1 Identify target companies (importers/distributors/building material suppliers) A focused list of qualified leads
2 I'll train them on what to say (how to present our products) Face-to-face introduction of our company
3 Collect senior manager contacts Direct access to decision-makers
4 Send free sample kits Buyers can evaluate quality first-hand
5 Arrange Zoom meetings Company intro, products, OEM/ODM capabilities
6 Invite them to visit our factory in China Build long-term partnership
Why This Works
  • Low Cost — No overseas trips or research firms
  • Real Feedback — Straight from buyers, not reports
  • Warm Leads — Personal visits open doors that emails cannot
  • Relationship-Driven — Personal connections matter in Africa
  • Scalable — Proven in one country, replicable across Africa
Budget Required
Transportation allowance + Lunch 200–250 yuan / visit
Sample kits for qualified buyers (2–3 kits) $50
Zoom meeting setup (free up to 40 minutes) No cost

Bottom Line
  • This is a low-risk, low-cost, high-impact approach to enter the African market
  • We gain real market feedback
  • We build direct relationships with local companies
  • We position ourselves for long-term growth in Africa
  • We can apply a similar approach to Central Asia, Turkey, and other markets
问题所在
  • 我们目前无法亲自前往非洲拜访潜在买家。
  • 仅靠冷邮件和远程调研，无法建立真正的信任。
  • 在全面投入销售资源之前，我们需要来自非洲市场的真实反馈。
解决方案 / 核心思路
  • 借助非洲当地可信的朋友/人脉，代替我们拜访目标公司。
  • 我们收集高管联系方式、安排产品样品，并组织线上会议。
我在博联的经验

在上一家公司（智能家居行业），我们曾面临一些挑战：

  • 我们的智能产品销往北美 / 欧洲 / 印度。
  • 我们需要来自不同地区的测试反馈（漏洞、连接故障等）。
  • 我委托在当地留学/生活的中国及外国朋友。
  • 我给他们提供兼职测试的工作。
  • 他们测试我们的产品，并直接向我们反馈问题。
执行步骤
行动 成果
1 确定目标公司（进口商/经销商/建材供应商） 一份精准的合格客户名单
2 我会培训他们如何表达（如何介绍我们的产品） 面对面介绍我们公司
3 收集高管联系方式 直接对接决策人
4 寄送免费样品套装 买家可亲自评估产品品质
5 安排 Zoom 视频会议 公司介绍、产品、OEM/ODM 能力
6 邀请其参观中国工厂 建立长期合作关系
为什么有效
  • 低成本 — 无需海外差旅或调研公司
  • 真实反馈 — 直接来自买家，而非报告
  • 优质线索 — 当面拜访，打开邮件打不开的门
  • 关系导向 — 非洲市场重视人际连接
  • 可复制 — 一国跑通，可推广至全非洲
