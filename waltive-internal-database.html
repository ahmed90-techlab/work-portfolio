Before the Fair Opens (Daily)

  • Review Quick Reference — memorize key FOB prices, MOQ, HS codes, and duty rates by region.
  • Charge phone and power bank. Download offline copies of this knowledge base.
  • Ensure sample boards are clean, labeled, organized by product family (SPC flat, SPC fluted, WPC fluted, mouldings).
  • Test your pitch: 30-second opening, 60-second product demo, 90-second value story.

During the Fair

  • Listen first. Ask "Where will this panel be installed?" and "Which country are you importing into?" before quoting.
  • Show, don't tell. Hand over samples. Let buyers feel the weight and edge quality.
  • Anchor to value. PUR lamination, certifications, complete system — not just price.
  • Capture contact details — name, company, country, product interest, quantity estimate.

After Each Meeting

  • Log the buyer's details within 15 minutes.
  • Send a WhatsApp follow-up with the specific product catalog and price list discussed.
  • Note objections — check Competitor Objection Scripts for better responses next time.

SPC vs WPC: Core Differences

Everything you need to explain why SPC is our flagship — and when WPC is still the right choice.

Key takeaway: SPC is better for wet areas, hot climates, and commercial projects. WPC is competitive on price for dry feature walls. We supply both — one supplier, one color, one container.

Raw Material Composition

SPC (Stone Plastic Composite): Calcium carbonate (stone powder) + PVC resin + stabilizers. Zero wood content.

WPC (Wood Plastic Composite): Wood fiber/flour + PVC resin + additives. Contains wood fiber.

Thermal Expansion

SPC has a much lower thermal expansion coefficient than WPC — roughly 30–50% lower. SPC boards are far more stable in hot climates (40°C+). WPC can warp under sustained heat.

Advocating SPC Over WPC — Script

"I understand — WPC fluted panels do look great for dry feature walls. But let me ask: where is this panel going to be installed?

If it's a hotel bathroom, kitchen backsplash, basement, or coastal project — SPC wins. Four reasons:

1. Weight & shipping cost. WPC grille panels are 1.2–1.5 kg/m; SPC is 0.6–1.1 kg/m. That's 30–50% lighter — more meters per container, lower freight.

2. Moisture & warping. WPC contains wood fiber and can swell or grow mold. SPC has zero wood — dimensionally stable forever.

3. Installation speed. Same click system, but SPC's lighter weight and rigidity install faster — especially on ceilings.

4. Fire performance. Both are B-s1,d0, but SPC's stone core adds inherent fire resistance.

Recommendation: SPC for wet/hot zones, WPC for dry feature walls. Same color, one container."

USA — Section 301 Tariff

  • Base MFN: 5.3%
  • Section 301: +25% (List 3 / List 4A — verify current status)
  • Total effective: ~42.8% landed
  • State sales tax added at point of sale
  • Business implication: USA remains a premium market but total landed cost is 3× higher than Australia or SE Asia

EU — CBAM (Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism)

  • Base MFN: 6.5%
  • VAT: 15–25% depending on country (DE 19%, FR 20%, ES 21%, IT 22%)
  • CBAM transition period runs through 2025; full implementation from 2026
  • Importers must report embedded carbon emissions
  • WPC panels may fall under CBAM scope — verify with EU importer

Australia — ChAFTA Advantage

  • Base MFN: 0% under ChAFTA (Australia–China Free Trade Agreement)
  • GST: 10% (creditable for GST-registered importers)

Quality Issues & Standards

Common quality issues, root causes, and Huating standards.

Common Quality Issues

1. Delamination (film peeling)

  • Root cause: Cold glue instead of PUR; insufficient surface treatment
  • Most common in: Budget Shandong factories
  • Huating standard: PUR hot melt + plasma pre-treatment

2. Warping in hot weather

  • Root cause: Excessively high filler ratio (70%+), low PVC resin
  • Huating standard: Controlled filler (max 65% SPC, 60% WPC), multi-stage cooling

3. Batch-to-batch color mismatch

  • Root cause: Poor color dosing, no final QC
  • Huating standard: Automatic masterbatch dosing, spectrophotometer verification

4. Edge brittleness and cracking

  • Root cause: Too much filler, insufficient impact modifier
  • Huating standard: CPE impact modifier, drop impact testing

5. Poor scratch resistance

  • Root cause: Thinner film than advertised (0.08mm vs 0.12mm), no top coat
  • Huating standard: Genuine 0.12mm film with scratch-resistant top coat
